# Stage 4 protocol v5.2-D.5 — scientific interpretation and triplet-first execution

September 18, 2026. This amendment incorporates [D.4](R1_stage4_protocol_v5_2_D_4.md), including its normative predecessors, except where explicitly superseded here by DEC-068 and DEC-069. It grants no draw, seal, signature or launch authority. Experimental completion remains October 9; presentation is October 15.

## Scientific question and scope

The present experiment tests whether a selected BP-trained feedforward learned reader, with support-taught per-position deltas and a frozen original language-model base, improves retrieval and generalization while preserving unrelated behavior. Primary v5 is compared with the random reader and stable baseline and, where registered, matched-update, live and continuation controls. It is not a test of the entire original predictive-coding programme. The co-trained base-by-cap factorial experiment, matched BP/equilibrium-PC comparison, and settled-cap branches are explicitly deferred. Earlier synthetic BP/ePC pilots are historical evidence, not completion of those branches. The optional historical-v2 comparison is secondary and does not isolate a single architectural cause.

D.4's 285 core cells, 45 optional cells, condition settings, independent populations, endpoint denominators, cap benchmark policy, continuation-certification gate, 63 primary metric intervals, and stopping rules remain unchanged. MQuAKE checkpoint 1,000 remains unavailable; its actual checkpoint-300 observations are descriptive. No missing or omitted result is imputed.

## Three-realization inference — DEC-069

Keep the DEC-057 percentile computation, nominal multiplicity allocation, seeds, thresholds and DEC-058 classifier exactly for traceability. Interpret their outputs as **preliminary decision summaries**. Display effect estimates before category labels. Every contrast must show each realization estimate and its five paired order differences, with within-realization minimum, maximum and sample standard deviation. Orders share a population and are not independent realization replicates. Missing orders remain missing; they do not acquire zero values or reduced denominators.

With three complete realization means, the registered percentile intervals reduce to the observed minimum and maximum. Under independent continuous symmetric realization errors, the range covers the population centre with probability 75%; that mathematical example is not an estimate of this experiment's coverage. Neither demonstrated 95% familywise error control nor an established population-level effect may be claimed from these three draws. The fixed 63-interval family remains intact, including all 21 unavailable MQuAKE intervals. A positive category requires a point RET-GS improvement of at least five percentage points and a lower registered bound above zero; it does not establish a five-point population improvement. Negative can mean failure to reach the registered margin, not necessarily measured harm.

Beside each complete registered contrast interval, display a **secondary, pointwise 95% Student-t sensitivity interval**, with 2 degrees of freedom: mean of the three realization means ± 4.302652729696142 × their sample standard deviation / sqrt(3). The 95% display convention specifies DEC-069's approved t sensitivity statement; it changes no classifier input or primary threshold. Label the assumptions: independent identically distributed normal realization errors, not checked with three observations. This is not a simultaneous interval, does not establish familywise coverage, and is not clipped to the metric's possible range. Missing or invalid paired populations make the interval unavailable. Zero observed between-realization variance produces a degenerate interval with an explicit caveat, not proof of certainty.

The measured concentration of harm within each fixed 245,237-position validation inventory remains a within-population result. Report recurrence across fresh realizations descriptively; positions and execution orders are not additional independent draws. Heavy-tail descriptions must distinguish concentration and rare large effects from evidence for a power-law distribution. The κ pilot remains a null/negative pilot, not an admitted κ treatment.

## Prospective locality construction amendment — lead approved September 18

The lead approved “deterministic exclusion and selection” in [the recorded request](tasks/R1-63o-locality-selection-approval.md) after a dry construction of the existing reservations exposed exact-text collisions. This explicitly supersedes D.1's prohibition on substituting confirmation locality rows after a collision. No treatment outcomes or model calls were consulted.

Traverse the same reserved outside items in sorted item-ID order, then their locality prompts in source order. Exclude exact matches to any reserved edit prompt or paraphrase in the complete stream, including edits after the first checkpoint; deduplicate exact text and select the first 50 eligible prompts. Record excluded source indices, eligible counts and any shortfall. Keep all 50 independently expected locality IDs, with missing rows if fewer than 50 eligible strings exist. Do not draw new subjects, change the seed, borrow another realization's items, filter by measured outcomes or reduce the denominator. The seal's complete-stream exclusion guard remains in force. This is an explicitly approved prospective construction rule, not retrospective alteration of observed endpoint results.
