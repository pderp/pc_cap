## What would support the continued-base explanation?

The margins are **DEC-033 D-R3**, while DEC-034(a) introduces this control: absolute RET-GS proportion `.05`, ES non-inferiority `−.02`, LS `−.01`; these are five, two and one **percentage points**, not relative percentages.

For each dataset let `S0` be original-base StableCap, `S1` continued-base StableCap, and `R0` the original-base revision learner. Report `S1−S0` (continuation effect), `R0−S0` (observed revision gain), and `R0−S1` (revision gain above continuation). A positive continuation effect that captures the observed gain, leaves residual RET-GS below `.05`, meets ES/LS non-inferiority and passes ordinary fidelity is **compatible with a continued-base explanation**. Also inspect the continued-base revision cell to detect a base×cap interaction. Require complete comparable budgets and matched items before that interpretation.

Point estimates alone do not establish equivalence or causal attribution. Use paired uncertainty intervals and later independent realizations; a single selected 100-edit development stream cannot certify the confirmatory claim. In particular, a no-op self-distillation result cannot exclude informative continuation, and a failed/nulling revision learner cannot make continuation “explain a gain” that is absent.
