`aw/pc_v1_readout.py`, plus the new metadata helper `aw/pc_treatments.py`, are

`aw/pc10_stage.py` reproduces the three reader transformations and the combined
patch; helper source lives in the candidate directory until application.

- Read requested and effective iteration/rate settings from config and finish,
  checking both against the plan. SE-A stays at its inert defaults but is labelled
  with the SE-E treatment it controls. Partial or inconsistent records are refused.
  Missing treatment fields in **historical default finishes** are admitted only
  with their eight-step/rate-0.1 configs; no nondefault setting is inferred.
- One `compare` call accepts one treatment. Multiple run groups produce separate
  treatment reports and a labelled sweep table, with no pooled arm or cross-treatment
  mean. Duplicate cells within a treatment remain errors, and missing cells remain
  explicit. The original primary/secondary scoring functions and within-treatment
  effect calculations remain intact.
- Each PC-v0 harm run still accepts one completed paired group. Reconstruction
  uses the recorded cap iterations and a base constructed at the recorded error
  rate, preserving checkpoint, state and weight verification. Group, cell, nested
  readout and pair outputs carry the treatment. Separate completed harm reports
  can be displayed in a CPU-only sweep table, with matching position inventories
  and no aggregation across treatments.
- Fixed-v5 `from_checkpoint` accepts approved 8/16/32-step snapshots and their
  rate, requiring config/finish agreement for nondefault snapshots. Its caller
  must construct EPCBase with that rate **before** any graphs are cached; wrong
  rates, arms, hashes or semantic states are refused. Legacy default calls retain
  the original interface.
- A sweep cannot be drawn as one pooled efficacy/cost plot. Individual treatment
  plots identify their settings. Nondefault reports and sweeps cannot overwrite
  `docs/additional_work/PC-v0_report.md`, the original experiment's report slot.
  Do not point the original slide-source register at a contingency child report;
  a new experimental result needs its own explicit presentation label.
