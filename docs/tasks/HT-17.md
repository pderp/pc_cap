## Deliverable

One report (`docs/additional_work/HT-17_report.md`) and a small figure set, from the saved per-position vectors only:

1. **Observable and population declared once.** Δ = NLL(cap) − NLL(cap-off) in nats at the same prefix (field 0 − field 1
   of `full-validation-1000.npz` / `harm/vectors.npz`), signed; benefits, near-zero and positive changes all retained.
   The gate's firing indicator reported separately where saved (checkpoint / report telemetry); Δ > u is a harmful-change
   indicator, not a firing rate. Distinguish exact zeros from floating-point noise and from clipping.
2. **Populations.** Stage-4 learned reader, stable v0 and random reader on the common text population (both datasets,
   every realization and order; the other v0-family comparators as a secondary table); the paired AW-B evaluation arms
   (ten memories × v5 / mixture / cap-off); the BP and ePC reader seeds as they complete (ePC seeds 1–2 land ≈ Oct 2
   08:45 and ≈ Oct 3 10:30); the 4,064-position pilot assay kept separate from the 245,237-position assay and labelled.
3. **Excess fits.** For thresholds u ∈ {0.01, 0.1, 0.5, 1} nats: exceedance probability P(Δ > u), the number of excesses
   and of distinct contributing windows, and a maximum-likelihood fit of the excesses Y = Δ − u | Δ > u to the
   generalized Pareto (location 0; `scipy.stats.genpareto`, shape `c` = Nelson's κ for α = 1) compared with its
   exponential restriction by predicted survival and held-out-window log-likelihood; if both fail visibly, one correctly
   normalised alternative (conditional lognormal) rather than the nearest family. A free-α (stretched) fit at most as a
   secondary sensitivity; cut first if time is short.
4. **Uncertainty with the dependence kept.** Resample window identities jointly across paired cells (the same 1,931
   window ids in every cell); report realization, order and reader-seed variation separately from text-window
   uncertainty; label the window bootstrap as conditional on the unverified independence of windows. Never treat the
   15 × 245,237 cell-position observations as independent.
5. **"Not identified" is an allowed answer.** Screening rule, stated as a reporting rule: fewer than 100 excesses or fewer
   than 30 contributing windows → counts and empirical curves only, no headline shape. Above the screen, report optimizer
   convergence, threshold stability and predictive fit; mark endpoint-boundary fits (shape ≤ −1) and shapes outside the
   manuscript's entropy domain (κ ≤ −1/2 for α = d = 1) as **invalid**, not as estimates (this applies to the AW-B
   mixture, whose 1-nat ceiling is proven by construction, not fitted).
6. **Outputs small.** Figure 1: harmful-change frequency against conditional severity per condition × dataset (with
   intervals). Figure 2: empirical survival with fitted curves and threshold sensitivity for the three main conditions.
   Optional: a shape-against-scale parameter plot labelled by population and threshold (not a map of universality
   classes). ES99+, maxima and efficacy stay beside the new quantities. **No calibrated-entropy column** in the empirical
   comparison; a separate half-day calibration demonstration on known distributions (normalised α = d = 1 densities,
   slope condition at x = σ, escort moment, κ → 0 limit; formula 1 + ln_{κ/(1+κ)} σ) is optional and stops if it becomes
   a library port.
7. **Language.** Fitted finite-range shapes, frequencies and severities; no complexity class, no W(N), no asymptotic law,
   no infinite-variance claim, no temperature. The analysis succeeds if it establishes differences *or* shows the family
   is not identifiable. These exposed evaluation tails tune nothing (no reader, κ or mixture selection).
