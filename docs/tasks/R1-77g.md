## Findings that change the handoff

1. **Bindings alone cannot change the factor.** The installed queue hardcodes
   1.15; the scheduler obtains it once at invocation. It also compares the matrix's
   frozen ceiling-contract dictionary to its installed constant. Editing that
   dictionary or changing a recipe ceiling would violate current admission.
2. **The bindings file is read once, then repeatedly hash-checked.** Replacing its
   contents under the running process makes `inputs_current()` fail; it does not
   reload policy. A versioned consumer and a new invocation are required.
3. **The maximum is 1.656925, not 1.64.** The latter approximates the mean ratio for
   v0_stable CounterFact. The measured maximum is 9,773.61 process seconds divided
   by its registered 5,898.64-second solo estimate. Factor 1.7 is only 2.60% above
   that observed maximum, before the retained 1.5 safety factor. These are ratios
   to a cost model containing reviewed transfers, not matched one-worker reruns
   of the same confirmation cells.
4. **Full-validation slowdown is not uniform or the sole demonstrated cause.**
   Mean full-phase/model ratios are 2.25 for primary zsRE but 1.27 for v0_stable
   CounterFact. Other classes range below and above one. Whole-cell cost pressure
   is established; a blanket causal claim of 1.6–2.4× full-phase concurrency
   slowdown across classes is not established by these records.
5. **The broader allowance exceeds the cap in the all-ceilings scenario.** At the
   historical block-1 boundary, 46.805 actual process-hours plus the remaining
   proposed ceilings total **893.912 hours**, versus 619.848 under v1. The shared
   cap stays 750; the scheduler still stops when actual charges plus current
   reservations cannot fit. This does not predict 894 hours of actual runtime,
   but it removes any implication that the proposed ceiling envelope guarantees
   all remaining cells fit. Applying 1.7 only to the two currently tight classes
   would instead give 639.916 hours in that scenario; that narrower alternative
   is **not** the prepared all-cell policy and would need a separate revision.

## Prepared implementation and operational procedure

The new [consumer](../../logs/r1_77g/consumer.py) uses the existing scheduler's
namespace interface; it does not edit or monkeypatch a bound module. It validates
the versioned amendment and resolves the unchanged v1 recipes, overrides the
two-worker allowance and remaining-ceiling forecast together, and records the
amendment and new resume authorization in new process receipts. The existing
matrix SHA and receipt root continue to govern cost/retry history, locks and cell
destinations. Actual older process charges are retained exactly. The native
lease, budget reservation, memory, deadline, checkpoint, retry and fidelity-watch
checks still execute. The new consumer requires a current clean D11 cutover
snapshot and an exact newly signed request before it can dispatch.

The v2 proposal deliberately uses an amendment-envelope schema with a bound
`base_bindings` reference, **without a legacy top-level `recipes` key**. The old
queue therefore rejects it before dispatch, instead of silently ignoring the new
factor. Its unsigned status remains immutable; a separate exact signed request
authorizes a future invocation. Inspection alone grants no authority.

The [resume runbook](R1-77g-resume.md) covers a controlled parent-only drain,
reconciliation of completed worker receipts and potentially pending watch updates,
fresh signing, preserved accounting, and a bounded next invocation. The old
step-9 signature does **not** authorize the different bindings or consumer and
must not be reused or overwritten. The new authorization references its
predecessor; original execution records remain intact.

After a future activation, ordinary D11/D13 outputs still calculate their frozen
1.15 projection. Their actual costs remain useful, but that projection must not
be presented as the active v2 allowance. The new read-only
[accounting supplement](../../logs/r1_77g/accounting.py) preserves canonical D11
bytes and adds the explicitly versioned 1.7 projection. It keeps unknown live
cost unknown and requires the saved signed resume authorization to label the
view authorized. Its [block-1 demonstration](../../logs/r1_77g/block1-accounting-scenario-final.json)
is explicitly an unsigned scenario. Integrating this display into a later final
report requires an explicitly versioned reporting change, not a silent rewrite
of D11's existing meaning.
