## Queue and cost

Two subprocess workers share the parent queue's exclusive GPU lease. Each child verifies the live parent PID, held lock, bound launch receipt, source identities and CUDA backend. Admission reserves host-memory headroom above **6,144 MiB**, including conservative prospective worker peaks. A GPU project-process occupancy check precedes dispatch.

Every dispatch has durable start/finish receipts in `logs/additional_work/R/queue/<session>/<cell>/dispatch-N/`. Finish repeats the start identity and binds its bytes. Whole-process time supersedes covered native-attempt time once; overlapping workers' process time is summed, not counted as elapsed wall time. Unknown/torn accounting is refused.

Per-cell ceilings use **1.7 × donor mean, once**. A retry consumes the remaining ceiling; it does not reset the cell budget. At most one retry is permitted, only after an ordinary cell failure with a certified checkpoint and verified closed journals. Host failure halts further admission; existing workers finish within their recorded bounds. Completed cells cannot be executed again. An unfinished queue session requires owner reconciliation rather than guessing elapsed spend.

Validated plan: **38.030 process-hours**, **64.651 summed ceiling-hours**. Deterministic two-worker FIFO estimates are **20.285 elapsed hours at donor means**, **34.485 at cell ceilings**. Overlap is an assumption, not a measured speedup. The original **30-hour wall portfolio allowance** is preserved and includes prior queue-session elapsed time; the queue stops if that or the October 9 cutoff is reached. Hence the complete matrix is not guaranteed within budget. GPU throughput remains unmeasured for this consumer.
