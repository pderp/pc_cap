and its retained-fields check omitted these execution settings. Consequently
`run_drift_assay` selects the scalar full-integrity path for v0. The original donor
used batching. The assay population has not changed; this is an execution-path
mismatch, with numerical parity needing validation before any repaired version.

  drift phases together took 288.22 seconds.

  did not produce a completed phase. No final full-validation phase completed.
- Original donor order 100 used 1,542.81 seconds for its separate full-validation
  pass. That further cost remained ahead of this cell at timeout.
- The completed R edit/retention phases are comparable to, or faster than, the donor's.
  Two-worker overlap occurred, but there is no controlled measurement separating
  contention from other costs. It does not explain away the documented scalar path.
- The fidelity watch runs **after** a successful child exits. It cannot account
  for this child's timeout; the full-validation endpoint had not completed either.

At comparable scalar rates, three drift passes alone would take about 6,174 s,
leaving only 1,828 s for all other phases and full validation under the 8,002 s
ceiling. The two completed edit/query groups already cost 1,377 s. This is an
estimate, not a forecast interval, but repeating this class unchanged is poor use
of the remaining budget. The missing fields also affect v0 CounterFact recipes;
its precise slowdown has not been measured here.
