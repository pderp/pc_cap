## Option B — executed-only exposure

R1-X9 found 751 subjects with no matched evidence under its audited query scope. Of these, 729 can add MQuAKE
capacity after independent reasons and zsRE priority; 22 can add zsRE capacity. “No matched evidence” is not proof
of no execution. There are eight historical training sessions with missing full metrics, bank receipts or cached
banks, conditional reconstruction of RNG schedules, unreviewed non-stream assays, and incomplete alias/context
mapping. Memory-support use is not fully covered by a query trace audit.

The 729 MQuAKE review candidates divide exactly as follows. Abbreviations: D = development primary reservation,
L = development locality query, U = development unrelated query. No training-reserved subject enters these

The 62 subjects with a primary development reservation need especially strong evidence: absence of a queried
own prompt does not establish absence from installed memory or model selection. True-query exceptions alone
would not release those primary reservations.

Bank-only observations remain exposure under this option unless the lead separately defines “executed” as
reader consumption. That different rule has a conditional ceiling of 2,999 subjects in
[the reader-only sensitivity](../../logs/r1_round11/reader_only_capacity_review.json), still below 4,050.
Do not combine that ceiling with the 2,829 count.

Even perfect clearance of the 729 subjects is insufficient for the full proposal. At 2,829, three realizations
allow at most 593 edits each with the existing reserves (2,829 exactly); two × 1,000 plus reserves requires
2,700 and leaves at most 129. Those remain scope amendments. Reconstructing missing execution records is useful
for audit quality, but is not the shortest route to preserving three full 1,000-edit realizations.

## Option C — exempt true-fact locality/unrelated presentation

This policy changes the estimand from unseen subjects to **new counterfactual targets on subjects that may have
appeared as preservation/null queries**. A defensible preregistration would specify exactly which roles are exempt,
retain every historical primary training/development subject, retain all independent exclusions, and report
previously queried and wholly unseen subjects separately.

The correct historical ceiling for that rule is **4,218**, not 4,818:
5,577 − 159 − 1,200 = 4,218. The script independently reproduces 4,489 items / 4,218 subjects by waiving only
the three query-specific exclusion reasons. The supplement's top-level
`primary_only_mquake_subject_ceiling=4818` is scoped to the new slices, despite its ambiguous name.
4,818 = 5,577 − 159 − 600 applies only to the prospective 500/100 slices. Reaching it retrospectively would also
release 600 historically primary-reserved subjects, which the proposed true-query exception does not authorize.

What was actually presented matters. The stored locality rows contain a neighbour's source `target_true`, but
`stream_train.build_bank` tokenizes its **prompt**, saves cap-off last-position logits and uses a null target.
It does **not** teacher-force that row's `locality_answers` as a ground-truth true answer. The reader can consume
the prompt features and null/preservation objectives; development evaluation generates cap-on/cap-off answers
and examines selection statistics. Thus “the reader saw the TRUE fact” is too broad: these roles show a
subject-bearing prompt and model distributions/outcomes, not necessarily a teacher-certified true answer.
Primary support construction, by contrast, includes the counterfactual answer and its answer-prefix features.

Reviewers could object that the reader learned subject-specific rejection or selection behavior, that answer
strings or aliases occur in other contexts, that repeated developer feedback influenced checkpoint choice, and
that this policy is being proposed after seeing a capacity shortfall and results. A new label and explicit
sensitivity analysis help disclose that change; they do not recreate unseen-subject confirmation.

Its 168-subject margin is only 4.15% of the 4,050 requirement, before further clearance. Even if accepted, the
joint eligible role allocation must be counted before any random draw. It is a scientifically different fallback,
not an administrative correction. I do not recommend it as the default solely to recover sample size.

## Option D — another source, including MQuAKE-T

The local bytes of MQuAKE-T, SHA-256
`58500a1d4aaf9e036c23a1d9dd6be2d73d693e35a695614842f211ea5aff59f2`, contain **1,868 cases**,
not the 1,825 advertised by the local upstream README/task list. All 1,868 have one requested rewrite:
they reuse just **96 distinct subject/relation edits and 86 normalized subjects**. There are 96 distinct edited
Wikidata triples, no conflicting new targets within a subject/relation, and 1,868 distinct case IDs.
Case count therefore greatly overstates possible independent editing capacity.

Exact normalized-label overlaps: 7 subjects with prepared MQuAKE-CF, 3 with its historical exposure union
(including 1 primary), 4 with current MQuAKE candidates, 8 with current CounterFact candidates and 2 with current
zsRE candidates. These overlap counts need not be disjoint. They are lower bounds on overlap pending aliases
and contexts. Even all 86 subjects would not close the 1,950-subject conservative deficit.

[The upstream README on disk](/home/derp/cap/assets/data/raw/mquake/README.md) identifies T as real-world temporal
updates and CF as counterfactual replacements. T's later true answer is not a counterfactual false replacement.
Treat T as a separately declared temporal population with time-specific old/new truth, provenance, extended
pre-edit answer aliases, deduplication and teacher eligibility. It is not automatically admissible to the adopted
MQuAKE-CF population under DEC-045. Replacing targets synthetically would create a new dataset/version needing
independent validation. Applying the base-wrong-new-answer eligibility rule alone does not change the semantics.

An additional independent counterfactual source could preserve the full scope if it supplies at least 1,950
additional cleared subjects plus clearance headroom and compatible role/dependency inventories. It needs a lead
source decision, license/provenance, exclusion and alias mapping, tokenizer/schema checks, teacher yield,
composition checks and a new register. Do not multiply MQuAKE-T's repeated questions into new edit subjects.
