# Q21 — proposed post-freeze execution allowance

**Prepared for the lead; not approved or applied.** This is a proposed operational
amendment to DEC-071, not a retrospective change to experimental results.

May we replace the two-worker multiplier **1.15 → 1.7** on the existing whole-cell
solo ceilings for **all 330 full-validation-bearing cells on future dispatch**,
using the hash-bound R1-77g consumer and v2 bindings, while retaining the frozen
matrix, recipes, model/scoring/populations, existing 1.5 solo safety factor, retry
history, 750 process-hour hard cap and October 9 deadline?

Approval would also permit an orchestrator-coordinated drain of the queue parent,
reconciliation while globally idle, and preparation of a **new exact signed resume
request**. It would not overwrite the original launch signature or retrospectively
change old process limits or charges. The original request does not cover the new
bindings and consumer; the new request must bind the actual cutover snapshot and
chosen `stop-after` block. Nothing here sends a signal or resumes a process.

The reason is measured cost pressure: primary zsRE reached 90.91% and v0_stable
CounterFact 96.05% of their admitted effective ceilings in block 1. The maximum
process/solo-model ratio was **1.656925**, rather than 1.64; 1.7 adds 2.60% above
that observed maximum before the retained 1.5 ceiling safety factor. Only nine
condition/dataset classes have block-1 observations. This is a prospective
allowance for other classes, not evidence that their slowdown has been measured.

**Budget consequence to accept explicitly:** using the historical end-of-block-1
snapshot, actual spend plus all remaining proposed ceilings is **893.912 hours**,
above the unchanged 750-hour cap. The queue will still refuse dispatch when
actual spent time plus active reservations and the next cell's reservation do
not fit. This ceiling scenario is not an expected runtime; nevertheless, full
completion under the cap is not guaranteed by the broader allowance. A larger
total cap is not proposed or implied.

If this scope is too broad, request a narrower revision limited to primary zsRE
and v0_stable CounterFact. That alternative gives **639.916 hours** in the same
historical all-ceilings scenario and affects 30 declared cells, 20 then remaining.
It is a separate scope choice: the present v2 consumer applies 1.7 to all 330,
so do not use the prepared command to enact the narrower option.
