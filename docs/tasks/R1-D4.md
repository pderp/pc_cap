## Mapping and source integrity

One item per NFKC/casefold/whitespace-normalized subject plus relation. Lowest case_id, then rewrite index, wins;
19 conflicting later new-target occurrences are recorded with both targets and source cases. No source old-target
conflicts occurred. The filled cloze is the edit prompt, target_new.str is the canonical answer, matching
new_single_hops supply aliases, and question/cloze variants merge only for the same chosen target and matched
source anchor. All items have matching alias provenance.

9,218 cases contain 16,835 rewrite occurrences and 7,236 unique subject/relation pairs on 6,501 subjects.
Excluding **924 subjects removes 1,193 rewrites**, leaving **6,043 items / 5,577 subjects / 37 relations**.
The task's “924 dropped” is a subject count, not an item count. Every dropped subject and rewrite is listed in
excluded_subjects.jsonl / excluded_items.jsonl under assets, with hashes/counts in the manifest.

Answers include the shared tokenizer's separator and newline convention. All round-trip and fit 32 tokens;
the actual range is 2–16. The full token and relation histograms are in the preparation report.
This differs from a tokenizer count of bare target text and is the correct support-answer workload denominator.

Every item has two same-relation, other-subject locality clozes with source target_true answers and two further
distinct near-miss candidates. These four neighbours are disjoint for that item, not globally reserved across
every item. Teacher agreement and final split compatibility are not assumed.
