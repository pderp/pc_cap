## MQuAKE scope and interpretation

MQuAKE's core conditions are **R1_learned_ff (primary v5), R1_nonlearned (random-reader floor), and v0_stable**, each at 300 edits × three realizations × five orders: 45 core cells. The other five MQuAKE conditions—matched_update, v0_live_C1, v0_live_C2, S1_LM and S1_literal—are **prospectively not run** under DEC-066. Calibration v3 has radius zero in all their banks, yielding a degenerate editing configuration. Record that calibration implication as such; do not fabricate per-cell outcomes, confidence intervals, measured fidelity, or completed-cell receipts for the omitted runs. In particular S1 continued-base versus original-base fidelity remains its own certification quantity; zero cap radius does not imply that two different base checkpoints have exactly identical predictions.

The 15 MQuAKE extension cells (historical learned-v2 reader) remain optional and unchanged. zsRE and CounterFact each retain their 120 core and 15 optional cells. Total: **285 core + 45 optional = 330**. Conditions omitted here remain in historical development records and the 27-row cost inventory, but contribute zero planned runs to the D.4 projection and never enter the execution queue.

MQuAKE draw demand remains **1,950 distinct subjects** over three realizations, with per-realization roles unchanged: 300 edits, 100 outside, 100 near-miss supports, 100 neighbours and 50 revisions. The corresponding zsRE/CounterFact demand remains 4,050 each. Retain DEC-062 family-coordinated allocation and multiple disjoint pairs per family. This is a prospective condition reduction, not reuse of omitted slots to draw extra subjects.
