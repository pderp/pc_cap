## Boundary uncertainty and occupancy

The winning unseen result is **10 fires among 100 prompts**. A descriptive 95% Wilson binomial interval is **5.52–17.44%**; the exact Clopper–Pearson interval is **4.90–17.62%**. Neither puts the upper endpoint below 10%. These are fixed-candidate binomial reference intervals, not intervals adjusted for choosing among 42 correlated candidates; deterministic development sampling and shared histories further limit their population interpretation. The observed rule remains satisfied as approved: do not retroactively replace it with a confidence-bound rule.

**No queried item IDs are shared between any pair of these three assays.** The latter two use different sections of the pool as edits consume earlier rows; the 100-record assay uses a different source. The percentages are similar, but they do not identify a flat occupancy-response curve. The 300-record assay would fail a newly imposed 10% point-estimate gate on that pool-source population; it does not invalidate DEC-050, which explicitly gates at 100 records on development prompts. “300 records” is memory size, not a sample size of 300 queried prompts.
