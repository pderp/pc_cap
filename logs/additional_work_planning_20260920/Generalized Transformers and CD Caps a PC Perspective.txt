What fitting a fixed architecture leaves out
1 Continual learning                        Learn a new domain and keep the old skill. Storing knowledge and still being
                                            able to reach it are separate things.

2 Learning during use                       Predict, adjust state, update weights and find new structure together, each
                                            on its own timescale.

3 Exchange of capabilities                  Hand a learned skill to another system as a small module that can be tested
                                            before it is trusted.

4 Neural–symbolic links                     Let a reasoner or a program supply typed evidence to the same workspace
                                            the network reads.

5 Creativity off-distribution               Build and test a new composition when the familiar computations fail.

04 · Generalized transformers and CD caps
Averaging too early loses the binding
“The captain is Mira. The cook is Leo. The captain is ____”

  Memory A                                  Memory B                Uniform average of the records
  captain – Mira                            captain – Leo           Encode each record as one-hot role plus one-hot name and
  cook – Leo                                cook – Mira             average. A and B give the same vector. Which roles and names
                                                                    occur survives; who holds which is gone, and no later nonlinearity
                                                                    can bring it back.




The binding survives if the layer routes selectively, or lifts a joint role-and-name feature before it aggregates. In the
framework, evidence arrives as a weighted collection that remembers its sources, and taking the barycenter is a
choice made in the open.
